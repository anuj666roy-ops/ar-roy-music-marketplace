Become an industry partner

Invite real professionals to request a listing. Verify their identity and obtain permission before publishing their name, logo or contact details.

Build the network responsibly

  • Reach out individually and respectfully.
  • Ask before publishing a person's details or company logo.
  • Verify company websites and business contact details.
  • Clearly label paid listings and sponsorships.
  • Never describe a person as an investor or partner without their permission.
LinkedIn contacts cannot be automatically imported just because you are connected. You can invite contacts using LinkedIn's own permitted tools, or ask them to submit this partner form themselves once a real backend is connected.
